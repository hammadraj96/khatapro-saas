Keyboard-Only Lightning Billing

Stop cashier cash drawer shortages. Day-end blind cash counting requires cashiers to enter physical cash before seeing system totals, flagging discrepancies to the exact rupee.

  • ✓ Detailed cashier audit log & shift handover
